## The 16-vs-17 composition-slice adjudication (the charter's question)

**The concurrent same-ino publish composition — rung 15's standing-red
gate — belongs WHOLLY to rung 17.** Two independent reasons:

1. **The race is per-INO; the clause is per-BLOCK.** The standing red
   is the delta-chain/full-save LAYOUT BASE racing under two publishers
   of one inode (design-mw-layout-versions §6's named residual on the
   S9 publish surface — the versioned publish reply + peer-aware
   re-base). Rung 15's own leg proves it with 4 MiB-ALIGNED halves —
   no block is ever shared, the B4/W1 clauses never fire, and the C8
   drift still lands (39,402 on this rung's from-zero pass). A
   fast-path screen on block sharing cannot close a publish race that
   needs no block sharing.
2. **The design row scopes rung 16 to the clause + pins + tax row**,
   and rung 17 owns the publish-side machinery (the authority
   assembler, `PublishCall::WriteExtent` schema 4, the demotion
   barrier, the retention law). Building any of that here would be
   improvising 17's design.

The leg's composition gate therefore **stays standing-RED naming rung
17** (message updated; the honest nonzero exit kept).

## Why the leg has NO live firing arm (stated, not dodged)

The mission's live shape — "a write overlapping a peer's grant lands
via the rewrite path with the counter moving" — is **structurally
unreachable pre-17 through product verbs**, by induction on the shipped
§9.2 acquire algebra: desired is outward-rounded to block alignment
(`block_align_out`), a grant clips only at other grants' walls (which
are themselves block-aligned, base case: empty table), and REQUIRED is
never trimmed — a required span crossing a live foreign grant WAITS
(conflict serialization), it does not split. So every live grant edge
is block-aligned, every own-covered write's block is solely covered,
and `span_range_shared` on a whole-block span cannot fire on a healthy
fleet. That is KD-MW-12's D1 statement made mechanical: pre-demotion,
nothing shares a block, so the common case is never taxed. The clause's
live firing venue arrives WITH rung 17's demotion barrier (its
red-first demotion row is where `overlay_ineligible_range_shared` first
moves on a fleet); the firing half of rung 16 is pinned in-process
against directly-minted sub-block grants — the post-17 shapes.

What the leg CAN honestly gate, it now does: the ledger-export check
(the row is INVALID if `overlay_ineligible_range_shared` is missing
from a member's stats inode) and the **zero-misfire columns** — on the
block-aligned ranged row both range-clause ledgers must stay 0 (§9.5's
MPI-IO law; movement = the finding-#2 class, own custody refusing
itself on every own-covered write of the stream).
