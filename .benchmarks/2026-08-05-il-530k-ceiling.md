## The assignment map (why 4-5 of 8)

The chain: fio process → 1 session (`shard = fd % il_sessions_default`,
one data fd per process) → daemon `handle_hello_msg` owner pick →
session pinned to ONE service thread forever → `service_loop(idx)` sets
the direct-drive LANE (`set_service_lane`) → governed submits ride
shard `idx % width` → that shard's `sqz-ipc-ddN` reaper serializes the
lane's CQE serves. Lanes:service-threads:sessions-per-owner is the
whole geometry, and it is decided at HELLO.

Two composable admission mechanisms produced the concentration:

1. **Locality confinement (the dominant term).** With placement active
   (the 2×16 field box), the pick was `numa_core::pick_owner` =
   lexicographic **(distance, load, index)** — distance strictly
   dominated. The session-node inference is the peer pid's LAST-RUN
   CPU at HELLO instant (`session_node_for_pid`); a 32-process fio
   fleet forks its workers microseconds before their first `open`, so
   fork placement clusters the inference on the parent's node. Result:
   ~every session's placement node = one node → confined to that
   node's owner subset `{0,2,4,6}` of the interleaved partition = **4
   of 8 lanes** (+1 for the occasional straggler whose HELLO-instant
   CPU had already migrated = the observed 4-5). The same-pid sibling
   rotation (`rotate_exec_from`) never engages here — 32 DISTINCT pids
   with one session each. The prior local repro venue was single-node,
   where the pick reduces to (load, index) and spreads perfectly — the
   "local hides it" shape, again.
2. **The count-to-insert admission race (secondary, structural).** The
   pick counted owners by scanning the session registry, but the
   INSERT landed later on the same ctl thread — 32 concurrent
   fleet-launch HELLOs could read stale counts and convoy onto low
   indices. Schedule-dependent balance, never pinned.

## The fix (`f7fafe27`) — balance is structural, no new constants

- **`numa_core::pick_owner` minimizes `(load, distance, index)`** —
  balance first (the ingest-economy drain-capacity law: width must
  engage before locality is worth anything), locality the zero-load
  tiebreak (an idle box still admits local; the same-node fleet fill
  order is `0,2,4,6,1,3,5,7` — all 8 engaged, pinned).
- **Reserve-at-pick owner-load ledger** (`owner_loads`): the pick and
  its accounting are ONE atomic act (release at teardown/refusal,
  closure pinned) — 16 concurrent establishes on a ceiling of 4 land
  `[4,4,4,4]` structurally, ×10 green. Ledger len = the existing
  derived service-thread ceiling; the now-unread `service_threads`
  field was deleted (no dead code).
- **The session arena binds to the CHOSEN owner's partition node**
  (memory follows thread): daemon-side passes stay node-local BY
  CONSTRUCTION whichever owner balance picks — the NUMA campaign's
  measured stage-1 posture (`numa_local_bytes` 1.000) is preserved,
  and a fleet no longer piles every arena onto one socket. Single-node
  / `SQUEEZEFS_NUMA=0` mounts are untouched end to end.
- **Gauge `ipc_session_owners`** (owners with ≥1 live session) — the
  admission-time face of `ipc_direct_shards`; stuck below the derived
  width under fleet load is this field signature by name.
- Unchanged: sibling rotation (inference input), owner partition +
  pins, spawn-on-bind, severance law, KD-7, libaio semantics, all
  refusal classes. No new env knobs.
