## 1. What landed

Under bit 17 **and** `SQUEEZEFS_SYMMETRIC_META=1` every DATA volume has a **floating allocation lease** whose holder carves **ranged block grants** out of a **durable per-volume allocation bitmap co-located with its ring**, a successor is granted the lease **only after the dead holder's home region is recovered** (the §5.5.1 ordering law), terminal frees / the grace ring / quarantine belong to the volume's holder, the membership plane is **sharded by home volume**, a symmetric appender past `T_self` **parks** (acks held, admission waiting, nothing poisoned) and reclaims under the successor's grace, and the maintenance coordinator is volume 0's manager with each mount judging the inode plane over the slots it leases. **`=0` and a bit-17-absent volume are the shipped posture exactly** (§2).

### 1.1 The data allocation bitmap (`src/data_alloc_bitmap.rs`)

- One bit per block per data volume; A/B page pairs exactly `alloc_ext.rs`'s (newest-valid-wins, generation, xxh3) with a 32-byte header carrying the **`vol_tag`** and its own magic (`KVDA`) — a page of another volume's bitmap, another index, or the meta heap's bitmap never verifies; 4,064 payload bytes ⇒ 32,512 blocks per page; **32 KiB per TiB** at the 4 MiB block (tie-tested).
- **Deltas are kind-4 (`TREE_ALLOC_RESERVED`) records with a `vol_tag` prefix — a 16-byte key** (`vol_tag BE ‖ block BE`, values `0x11` SET / `0x12` CLEAR) beside the heap's 8-byte extent keys: the length is the ONE discriminator, consulted by `alloc_ext::load_inner` and the declared regions' replay before they decode (the journal's `Extent` detectors already ignore a key that fails the 8-byte decode).
- `DataAllocBitmap`: lock-free words, `set/clear/set_run/clear_run`, `first_clear_run(floor, want)` (the carve), `highest_set` (the recovery floor's bitmap term), `take_dirty_plan` / `write_dirty_pages` (the A/B alternation, `uring_fs::write_at_batch`), `region_image` (the successor's COPY — every page into slot A at one generation), `replay` (per-key LWW by record seq, then apply — idempotent), `drift(referenced, granted)` (the C6/C8 oracle: LOSS = referenced ∧ clear, LEAK = set ∧ ¬referenced ∧ ¬granted; `note_drift` counts the loss half on `data_alloc_bitmap_drift`).
- **The kept window** (§6 item 1): `note_replayed_delta` / `take_replayed_deltas` — the mount's own journal replay keeps every data delta it meets, by volume, until the recovery arm applies it.

### 1.2 Ranged block grants (`src/block_grant.rs`)

- `BlockGrant { start, len }`; `G = clamp(2 × ewma_milli_blocks_per_s × T_renewal_ms / 10⁶, 64, cap / (2 × writers))` with the floor 64 = one write-pipeline BDP window of 4 MiB blocks and a cap that never drops below the floor (a tiny volume grants the floor and the carve truncates at what is free); `T_renewal` = `membership::renewal_beat_ms()` (the installed session's interval, else the owner's, else the derived default).
- `BlockGrantLedger` (the HOLDER, mutex-serialized — the grant cadence, never the write path): `carve` = the lowest run of clear bits at or above the ledger's grant frontier (the zombie floor — every carve after an open grant starts at or above its END), bits SET on the bitmap at the carve; `Already` verbatim for a writer whose unconsumed remainder covers the ask (§5.3.5); `return_blocks` clears an unconsumed range the ledger holds for that writer (a range it does not is refused, nothing cleared — the bounded-execution law); `revoke_dead` takes a dead writer's grants out for the S7 quarantine (bits stay set); `open_ranges` / `grant_frontier`.
- `GrantWindow` (the WRITER): lowest-first mint (a refused mint never advances), `wants_topup` at 50 % of the last grant, `unconsumed` / `drain` for the leave. `BlockAllocator::install_block_grant_arm(vol_tag, sink)` + `install_block_grant` make `next_fresh_block` mint from the window; an exhausted window asks the sink once and retries, then refuses `StorageFull`-class (never a poison, never `note_journal_failure`); the unarmed allocator is the shipped loop verbatim (one `OnceLock` probe).
- `install_free_target` / `free_target_for`: the endpoint a co-writer ships a data volume's terminal frees to — consulted once per free group in `cowriter::ship_displaced_frees`, the set authority otherwise.

### 1.3 The lease, the ledger's records, the executors (`src/meta_backend/kv/alloc_lease.rs`)

- Tree 0 of VOLUME 0: `alloc_lease:{vol_tag} → { holder (KD-MW-2 identity), holder_appender_id, home_vol, control_ino, blocks, term, bitmap: [(home_vol, ExtentRef)] }` (v1, refuses a future version); `dead_member:{node_token, mount_slot} → { epoch, ts_ms }`; `recovered:{node_token, mount_slot, vol} → { by_term, ts_ms }`.
- `manager_alloc_lease_acquire`: none ⇒ term 1; the holder's own ⇒ `already`; a LIVE holder (no `dead_member:`) ⇒ `Busy` (`manager_verb_refusals`); a DEAD holder without `recovered:{holder, home_vol}` ⇒ **`KvError::LeaseDeferred`** (new, EAGAIN — the wire's `STATUS_DEFERRED`; nothing written); with it ⇒ `term + 1`, the record naming the predecessor's pages for the copy. `manager_alloc_lease_bitmap` (the holder at `term` publishes its pages — idempotent), `manager_alloc_lease_release` (a stale term is a replay), `manager_record_recovered` (idempotent; `recovered_records`), `record_death` (the in-process writer of the death record — PR 10's driver is the production writer; the wire refuses).
- The holder half: `hold_alloc_lease(vol_tag, blocks, term, predecessor image)` claims `region_len / node_size` extents from the manager's own bitmap (the alloc deltas journaled in ring 0 like every claim of its own), writes the region image (fresh, or the predecessor's COPY), journals, registers the `AllocHolding` (bitmap + ledger + pages + the ORDERED delta stream — round 2, §8.1); `holder_block_grant` = carve (bits SET + deltas queued in one critical section) → `drain_deltas` (ONE control entry per 1,024 deltas, in order) → **barrier** → reply (a journal failure gives the bits back); `holder_return_blocks`; `note_finish_free` (the allocator's `publish_free_list` hook — the bit clears and its CLEAR is queued under the same lock, the single-flight journaler lands it at once); `write_data_alloc_pages(ckpt_seq)` from the checkpoint task BEFORE barrier #1 beside the meta bitmap's page write, for the holdings homed on this backend only; `replay_data_alloc_deltas(bitmap, term)` = the kept window of that (volume, term, home) + a `JournalRing::recover` of ring 0 from the ledger's tail, folded once for that term; `read_alloc_bitmap_image(refs, blocks)` (this volume's pages — a foreign home volume's reader is PR 10's).
- `note_vol0_ledger_probe(reachable, now, T_owner)` — PR 3's `manager_should_release_role` decision WIRED: past `T_owner` unreachable the manager lease reads `vacant`, `manager_vol0_unreachable` counts, the coordinator predicate refuses; the D0 claim stays (the successor ladder is PR 10's).
- The coordinator predicate `symmetric_coordinator_refusal()` (volume 0's manager, registered at the routed open by `arm_symmetric_roles`, withdrawn at the leave) consulted FIRST by `jobs::maintenance_coordinator_refusal`; the lessee shards `inode_plane_owns_slot(local)` (`LeaseGate::verdict_structural` — leased, or unleased on the manager) gating C9/C10's candidates at their four nomination sites (`inode_plane_foreign_slot_scoped`), `inode_plane_slot_coverage()` (`covered ≡ leased + unleased`, `foreign` = another lessee's) summed into `fsck_inode_plane_slots_covered`.

## 6. Issues found on the way (attributed before any harness bound moved)

1. **The window's data deltas were LOST to the remount's own bring-up checkpoint** (this PR's plane, found red by the headline contract: `replay_data_alloc_deltas` changed 0 bits): a crashed holder's SET deltas sit in ring 0's window; the successor's open replays ring 0 (the meta-heap decoders skip the 16-byte keys) and then runs its bring-up checkpoint, which advances the ledger tail PAST the window before any allocation lease is re-held on the volume — so a recovery arm that scanned the ring from the ledger's tail found nothing, while the pages on the device still showed the granted range FREE. The mount's replay now KEEPS every data delta it meets (`note_replayed_delta`, by volume) and the recovery arm folds the kept window with the live ring scan once (LWW by seq). PR 10's driver — which replays a DEAD peer's ring rather than its own — reads the same arm; the note for that rung must keep the kept window's law where the recovering manager's own ring is the dead holder's (a manager recovering its predecessor's region 0).
2. **The grace-ring closure contract's first shape read a plane fact as a defect**: ring A's at-cap harvest FENCES the only reader (the pressure valve's forced progress), which unbounds the plane — ring B's next harvest is then routine and releases everything. The contract orders ring B's routine (empty) harvest before ring A's forced one and asserts ring B's later release carries NO timeout deferral; the closure holds per ring either way.
3. **The loom model's first form over-constrained the Dekker pair**: a committer that registered in flight, was COUNTED by the parker, then read `PARKED` and withdrew is a legal schedule (the parker holds acks for entries that never land — harmless; the lane holds what lands). The invariant is the other direction — never `Admitted` with `seen == 0` — which the model pins; weakening the fences is the schedule it reports.

### 8.1 The durable-truth core

- **Issue 1 (bug) — the page write's position.** The data bitmap's pages were written AFTER the ledger record whose tail passed their deltas; a kill between the two lost every SET below the new tail with the pages stale ⇒ a successor carved inside a live grant.
