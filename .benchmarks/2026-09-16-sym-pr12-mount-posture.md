## 6. What this rung does NOT contain — the stop-and-report

**The N-daemon backend posture** — a SECOND RW daemon on the same volume: a `KvMetaBackend` open that takes no flock and no claim, replays the manager's ledger + tree 0 as a PROJECTION, JOINs over the wire (`JoinAppender` → a region of its own), `AcquireSlots` over the wire, commits into ITS ring under the commit door with its leased slots, runs its own checkpoint (its page — the Issue-31 words — its slot trees' flushes, its SMOs in its ring under its grant), replays its own ring at reopen (own residue), reads foreign slots under tokens (PR 9's deviation 4), serves its slots' tokens / custody / shipped steps on its listener, and acts on `slot_release_notices` (the wire holder's flush-then-transfer). Design row 12 costs it at three weeks; it is the piece of row 12 left standing, and every seam marked "N-daemon" in §2 is its dependency. Until it lands, a second RW mount of an armed set meets the D0 gate's `FreshForeign` refusal exactly as before — nothing half-joins (no `Live` page is left for the manager to recover). This is a stop-and-report per the brief's last rule: the rung is PR 12's own, not PR 13/14's (measurement / deletion); the orchestrator decides whether it is PR 12b or folds into PR 13's venue work.

## 7. Deviations from the design, stated

1. `SQUEEZEFS_MW_BIND` is not retired (§6.1 retires four knobs; the brief's list named it) — every writer serves, the bind is where; `off` is refused on an armed set.
2. The block-grant top-up stays a pull (§2's measured reason).
3. The served `MarkShared`'s guard is the W1 site's `BLOCK_FLUSH_LOCKS(global ino, block index)` — the design's "4a guard" names the I{} lease; the patch site holds the block guard, so the block guard is what closes the window.
4. The reader's per-holder planes are keyed by LISTENER, not by appender id (the recall-channel law).
5. The ladder's rung 3 arms membership at `auto` on an armed set when no bind is declared — a behaviour change for the ARMED shape only (an unarmed mount with no bind arms nothing, as before).
