## S7-a — the device-rejection row (spec R2, scoped)

Fleet fresh; p0 device truth per data namespace read through the
writer's own controllers: `rtype=3 regctl=1`, one zombie key
(`0x2d5a0b13…`) on both namespaces (ONE WeroHold, one key — the shared-
hold law). Sustained buffered write load (dd 1 MiB blocks, conv=fsync)
running when the freeze landed.

* **Freeze past TTL**: SIGSTOP the armed writer (T_owner=15 s). The
  reader observed the frozen owner and **self-fenced by its own clock**
  (`membership_self_fences` 0→1 exactly; the S6 owner-death law — the
  member fail-stops before any re-grant could exist), then kept
  re-presenting (finding #1's fix) until the owner answered.
* **PR preempt observed on target**: recovery identity registered +
  preempted the zombie's key on BOTH data namespaces; post-preempt
  reports re-read: zombie key ABSENT, recovery key holds WERO,
  `regctl=1`, `rtype=3`. Recovery paths dropped before resume (merged-
  head kernel: the resumed zombie's I/O must ride its own association).
* **SIGCONT — the device rejects**: the zombie's resumed DMA failed with
  the reservation-conflict errno class (EBADE / os error 52) in its own
  log, and the zombie **FAIL-STOPPED**: `writer guard FENCED` latch line
  + `data-plane custody POISONED` + `data_dma_fence_refusals=2` (every
  post-latch submission refused and counted). The split held:
  `data_dma_epoch_refusals=0` **by construction** — nothing advanced the
  zombie's custody generation (no term bump, no revoked grant in its
  process); the fence here is the poison latch, and the gate asserts
  `epoch ⊆ fence` explicitly.
* **Quarantine law (scoped)**: `dlm_quarantined_offsets=0` and
  `dlm_quarantine_releases=0` on every member — nothing at this rung may
  hold offsets and nothing was released without a drain proof (the
  offset-holding cohorts are rungs 9-10; laws pinned in
  `tests/dlm_data_fence_tests.rs`).
* **Blast radius**: fence/epoch refusals moved on the victim ONLY;
  `data_plane_fence_mode=0` on the reader; `invariant_tripwires=0` and
  R5 columns (backstops, parked-gate timeouts) flat fleet-wide.
* **The S6→S7 handoff race, stated honestly**: with finding #1 fixed,
  the fenced reader re-presents FRESH the instant the resumed owner
  answers — a fresh JOIN replaces the dead lease before the TTL sweep
  can expire it, so the resumed owner mints a dead epoch only when its
  sweep wins that race. The leg accepts EITHER outcome loudly (this run:
  the re-join won; the mint arm is rung 7's proven S6-b/S6-b′ row, and
  pre-fix runs of THIS leg showed it live: `custody dead-epoch:N
  declared DEAD` on eviction).
* **Restore**: zombie killed (a fenced holder is dead until remount),
  recovery reservation released + unregistered, writer remounted fresh
  (`data_plane_fence_mode=1` — a fresh WERO under a fresh key over the
  released namespace), reader re-joined. **S7-a GATE GREEN.**
