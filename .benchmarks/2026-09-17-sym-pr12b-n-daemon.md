## 7. Deviations from the design, stated

1. The joiner's endpoint travels at rung 7 as its own verb (`PublishEndpoint`), not as "the joiner's endpoint word at `JoinAppender`": the listener does not exist at the open (rung 5 runs inside it, rung 7 after).
2. The manager's `ExtentGrant` idempotency is kept (the page's grant word is the witness); the JOINER keeps the word current — written before every ask and after every carve, no barrier (a crash between the record and the page write leaves C13's claimed-unreachable class, never an empty grant).
3. A joiner's frees SHIP to the holder through PR 9's per-holder custody lease (the co-writer's set-authority client does not exist on a joiner) — the verb and the owner half are S9's, verbatim.
4. The token divert on a writer reads LOCALLY when no custody arm exists (the in-process fixtures' default; the mount path always arms) — the honest posture for a backend with no wire, stated in the code.
5. The slot-lease carriage's member side spawns its work off the renewal (a release cycles the checkpoint) — the design's "the member runs flush-then-transfer" as a spawned act.
