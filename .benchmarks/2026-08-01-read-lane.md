## 3. What shipped (design deltas — docs/design-read-path.md §5.5 amendment)

1. **The hold** (`ReadLaneHold` on `TieredCache`) — **the default-on
   win**: a ledger-invisible, **purge-integrated** (R-6: the
   `purge_block_key` fifth arm), **coverage-retired** completed-fill
   store. Deposits: > 256 KiB demand primaries + pinned-lane fetches,
   inside the validated-fill window only (`Prefetch`-class excluded —
   R2's landing + spiral detector verbatim). Serves: the single-block
   fast path (binding-rechecked like hot/tier hits) + the single-flight
   loop's anti-refetch probe; credits at every consumption path retire
   entries at full coverage — memory converges by consumption. Budget:
   the derived consume-behind window (4 × streams × depth × block,
   floor 4 blocks, R5 `/8` cap), cached fast-up / ⅛-per-epoch-down.
   R5: `read_lane_hold` (sheddable trim) + `read_lane_inflight`
   (non-sheddable drain; Red ⇒ zero new speculation/deposits).
2. **Classified-membership tolerance** (the qd-reorder wedge): lane
   run-BUILDING stays exact-contiguity (the pinned random-vs-stream
   discriminator); a classified fresh lane additionally accepts members
   within ±64 × request_len of its edge, max-forward advance. Without
   it one libaio completion swap wedged the lane forever (arrival
   simulation: 0.7 % matched at qd8; field: 1,233 declassify events /
   70 s). 4 KiB ⇒ ±256 KiB: every 2026-07-26 random harm shape stays
   foreign (simulated 0 % member), governor DECLASSIFY suite green.
3. **The ahead lane** — **opt-in only** (`SQUEEZEFS_READ_LANE_DEPTH`,
   default 0): pinned-depth ledger-invisible whole-block fetches where
   R2 declines at zero share (single-flight-registered, reader-tied
   horizon, per-file single issue owner, R5-capped, Red ⇒ 0).
4. **The lever**: `SQUEEZEFS_READ_LANE=0` = exact prior behavior (the
   A0 attribution control, contract-pinned byte-for-byte).

## 4. The falsifications (counted — the charter's iterate-by-instrument law)

The charter's targeting hypothesis — *pipelined whole-block fetches ≥ 2
deep per stream ⇒ ~2× the plateau* — was **falsified on this venue**,
twice, by A/B rows on the same store:

* **Round 2 (pure-BDP depth)**: the write campaign's 2026-07-29
  self-fulfilling-equilibrium conviction reproduced on reads — measured
  lane bandwidth × measured latency floor targets the CURRENT plateau:
  `read_lane_depth_target` sat at floor 2 (32+ streams) and a
  depth-8 pin changed nothing (25.72 vs 26.28 GB/s).
* **Rounds 3–5 (engaged ahead-issue, AIMD-window and pinned depths
  2/4/16)**: every engaged row LOST to the hold-only posture —
  21.07/20.87/20.79/19.33 GB/s vs 25.98 (hold-only) at qd8; read_amp
  1.15–1.27 vs 0.71. Mechanism (counter-named): ~650 GB/row of
  ahead-fetched deposits died FIFO-unconsumed
  (`read_lane_hold_evicted_unconsumed` ≈ lane fetch bytes) — on a
  venue where the client's own qd × streams (256 × 1 MiB in flight)
  already covers the fabric BDP, ahead speculation only races the
  demand cohort's perfect single-flight amortization and throws away
  device bandwidth.

The falsified machinery was **deleted** (no dead code): the BDP
estimate plumbing and the AIMD-depth coupling are gone; the pin
remains as the high-latency/low-qd fabric lever pending an
engage-governor follow-on (§7).

## 7. Acceptance adjudication (honest)

* **Headline bar (≥ 0.8× matched raw ≈ 33.8 GB/s): NOT MET.** Landed
  25.7/26.1 GB/s sustained = **0.61×** raw (from 0.53×). The residual
  is a **per-op latency chain, not a concurrency term**: the qd8 row is
  Little-closed (256 × 1 MiB in flight / 10.4 ms clat = 24.6 GB/s ≈
  the row), and the raw same-shape control runs the identical in-flight
  at 6.2 ms. The FS adds ~4 ms/op on this venue — and adding fetch
  concurrency (the charter's hypothesis) measurably cannot buy it back
  (§4): the next campaign is the **read-serve latency chain**
  (whole-block fetch RTT decomposition: 4 MiB DMA + slice-out copy +
  binding resolution + FUSE round trip), the read twin of the
  write-side phase-residence program.
* **qd32 read_amp ≤ 1.05: NOT fully met** — 1.11 (from 1.41; the bar's
  distance closed 87 %). Residual: hot-tier + hold FIFO skew under 38
  interleaved streams still loses a few % of cohorts; the residual
  refetches are visible as `hold_evicted_unconsumed` > 0 on the armed
  legs. Named lever if the fleet wants the last 6 points: per-stream
  hold sub-queues (FIFO per lane, not global).
* **Both directions beat the A0 control by double digits with the
  amp collapsing toward 1** — the campaign ships as a strict
  improvement with the acceptance bars honestly marked unmet and the
  charter's mechanism half-falsified (the charter's own stop-rule
  case: the true residual mechanism is captured above with counted
  evidence for the follow-on charter).
