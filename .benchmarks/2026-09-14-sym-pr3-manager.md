## 1. What landed

Under bit 17 the writer that wins the D0 ladder on a volume IS its **manager** (KD-SYM-3 — an ordinary appender with the `is_manager` bit; the fixed ledger stays role-bound to it). It carves **extent grants** for every other appender, takes them back at the checkpoint cadence, answers **`JoinAppender`** over the S8 owner service, and on a PR substrate holds the metadata namespace under **WERO** with every other appender a registrant. With a grant, a declared region's structure — interior flips, allocator deltas — journals into **its own ring**: PR 2's deviation 3 is reversed and PR 2's `Lease` / `Extent` classes are the tripwires. Everything stays **dark** behind bit 17 and the two seams; a bit-17-absent volume is untouched except for two shipped-bug fixes (§6 items 1–2).

### 1.1 Grants (`src/meta_backend/kv/slot_state.rs`, `appender.rs`, `backend.rs`)

- **Durable form**: `extent_grant:{appender}` in tree 0 — the WHOLE grant as normalized runs (`ExtentGrantRecord`, versioned, total decode, ascending non-adjacent runs); the appender's page `grant` runs = the UNCLAIMED remainder (≤ `GRANT_RUNS_MAX` = 4; a remainder in more runs keeps its lowest — the rest are C13 candidates).
- **RAM** (`RegionGrant`): `unclaimed` / `claimed` sets, `pending (extent, gate)` parked on the REGION's tail, `returnable`; claim lowest-first; `free_pending` → `advance_durable(tail)` → `take_returnable` → `ReturnExtents` at the cadence; `recover(record, page)` at open, then the region ring's in-window `alloc` / `free` fold (`claim_exact` / `free_exact`); closure `granted ≡ claimed + pending + returnable + returned + unclaimed` published as `extent_grant_extents` / `extent_grant_{claimed,returned,unclaimed}`.
- **Sizing**: `grant_extents_derived = clamp(2 × ewma_smo_rate × failover_bound_s, 8, free_heap / (4 × appenders))` — the floor 8 = ≤ 2 extents per compaction/split × 4 pending root swaps per cycle; `SQUEEZEFS_SYM_GRANT_EXTENTS` explicit-wins; tie-tested (`sym_manager_grant_and_failover_bound_derive_from_the_ladder_and_the_smo_rate`). Refill proactive at 50 % (`refill_due`, `grant_cadence`) AND reactive: a flush pass that meets `GrantExhausted` with the manager reachable refills and retries the node once (§6 item 4).
- **The manager's executors**: `manager_extent_grant` (USER-class claims from the bitmap, ≤ 4 runs, alloc deltas + the merged record as ONE ring-0 control entry via `write_control_entry`, the page rewritten), `manager_return_extents` (free deltas at retire 0 + the record rewritten/deleted, idempotent → `(cleared, already)`), `manager_join_appender` (§1.3). `grant_cadence` per checkpoint: fold the SMO rate, ship returnable batches, refill at 50 %.
- **The stall arm**: `SmoContext::claim_internal` scoped to a region answers `KvError::GrantExhausted` → `SqueezefsError::refused(EAGAIN)` on the commit path; the flush pass DEFERS the node and counts `manager_dependency_stalls` (never `ENOSPC`, never `heap_full`); the heap admission refuses `GrantExhausted` only under the unreachable-manager seam (`test_set_manager_unreachable`) — otherwise it admits and the cadence refills (§6 item 5).

### 1.2 Own-ring structure (deviation 3 reversed)

`SmoContext` carries `region: Option<SmoRegion { appender_id, ring, grant }>` and a slot → region **resolver**; every SMO entry point (`smo_replace`, `try_merge_node`, `collapse_root_chain`, the lazy mint's `create_inner`) calls `scope_to(self.forest_slot)` first, so the flush pass, threshold maintenance, the merge sweep and the defrag arm can never run a leased slot tree's SMO in the manager's ring by omission. The entry reserves in the REGION's ring (`smo_ring`), claims from the REGION's grant, parks the retired image on the REGION's tail (`free_pending(extent, gate, forced)`). Per-slot dying floors for INTERIOR nodes and root swaps (`NodeCache::note_slot_dying_floor`, `KvTree::note_root_swap_floor`) clamp the region's tail; the checkpoint folds `SlotTrees::unpublished_root_floors()` PER SLOT and every slot-stamped node of a leased slot into that region's live floors. Replay phase 1 of a region ring applies its own interior records (level DESC) and folds its alloc/free deltas into its grant; `detect_appender_violations(rings, leases, granted)` makes an interior record legal for a leased slot and an alloc delta legal inside a grant.

### 1.3 The verbs (`src/meta_ship/manager.rs`)

`ManagerCall::{JoinAppender{identity, ring_want_bytes}, ExtentGrant{appender_id, want}, ReturnExtents{appender_id, runs}}` / `ManagerReply::{Joined{appender_id, page_addr, ring_segments, grant, already}, Granted{runs}, Returned{cleared, already}, Refused{reason}}` in bounded bincode frames (`MANAGER_SCHEMA` 1, the CONTROL class cap inside the body) as ONE verb family `VERB_MANAGER_CALL` on the S8 owner service; **`CLUSTER_WIRE_SCHEMA` 4 → 5** (bumped ONCE for this program's wire — KD-7 same-commit fleets; PR 4+ extend the enum). `ManagerService: RpcAsyncService` decodes, gates the schema, dispatches to the executors and records `manager_service_ns` {admit, execute, reply, total} exact-sum + the ledger (`manager_verbs` / `_per_s` / `_replays` / `_refusals`, `manager_load_pct` = Σ service ÷ wall). Idempotency is the DURABLE witness: a `JoinAppender` re-sent to this manager or to a SUCCESSOR answers `Joined{already}` off the joiner's `Live` page; a return of extents the record no longer grants answers `already`. `manager_join_appender`: the lowest `Free` directory page or the chain grown by one extent (header LAST, then the previous header's `next`, each barriered), a ring of ≤ 8 coalesced extents (alloc deltas as one control entry), the page `Live` into BOTH directory slots (PR 2's directory-first law), the initial grant; refuses past `appenders_capacity` naming the metadata volume COUNT (R-SYM-6). `ManagerClient::{join, extent_grant, return_extents}` is the caller side. The join refusal at open is narrowed to a `Recovering` page or a foreign `Live` page whose id THIS mount's declared partition claims — a wire joiner's page is a peer.

### 1.4 The role, the fence posture, the registrant ceiling

- **`manager_lease`** = `held` (this mount won the ladder) / `peer:<node token>` (another node's `Live` page 0 — a probe's face) / `vacant` (no forest, no set); **`manager_failover_bound_ms`** = `CLIENT_STALE_TTL_SECS × 1000` + the ladder's measured wall + the replay's measured wall, DERIVED at open, published, ALSO `manager_dependency_stall_bound_ms`. A successor that wins over a dead manager's `Live` page 0 adopts its newer roots at replay (the fixed-ring replay IS the dead manager's ring) and takes the role. `manager_should_release_role(unreachable_ms, T_owner)` is the §5.5.2 decision as one function + the `manager_vol0_unreachable` gauge (PR 8/10 wire the roles).
- **KD-SYM-13** (`decide_meta_fence_posture` at open): a declared partition or a join on a namespace without PR refuses unless `SQUEEZEFS_SYM_ALLOW_NON_PR=1` (announced "detection-grade" at every mount using it); a solo forest mount arms nothing and keeps the shipped rtype-1 posture verbatim.
- **§5.8.1 WERO**: with the plane armed on a PR substrate, `SQUEEZEFS_META_PR_WERO=1` (default) makes the manager's hold rtype 3 (`meta_pr_wero` = 1; `rsv_acquire` / preempt / release rtype-aware) and joining appenders REGISTRANTS (`data_custody::join_wero_as_registrant` — the standing hold one namespace class over); `=0` keeps rtype 1 and is refused on a PR substrate without the opt-in. One identity per mount; no fencing groups, no derived identities, no target-ACL probing. Co-located appenders sharing a registrant are their own guarantee row (KD-SYM-22 — `flock` + `T_self` + the frame screen; `operations.md`).
- **KD-SYM-18/23** (`reservation.rs`): the Reservation Report read is two-step SIZED BY REGCTL (`report_len_for`: header, then `header + 64 × REGCTL`, re-read once if the count grew) on both namespace classes; `parse_reservation_report` is total; the cap is DECLARED (`SQUEEZEFS_PR_REGISTRANT_CAP`) or LEARNED from the first non-conflict REGISTER refusal (`learn_registrant_cap`, min); `registrant_cap_gate` refuses a join LOUD at or past it naming the count, the namespace and the remedy (`pr_registrant_cap_refusals`). Gauges `pr_registrants_per_namespace` / `pr_report_bytes` (per device path), `pr_registrant_cap` (0 = unbounded, nvmet's posture).

### 1.5 fsck C13 (`src/fsck.rs`, `KvMetaBackend::c13_orphan_image_extents` / `c13_return_orphan`, `KvTree::reachable_node_addrs`)

An extent an appender's grant holds CLAIMED that no tree root reaches. The census runs under the SMO mutex AND the forest's mint guard (`SlotTrees::mint_guard`), so an SMO's successor before its route flip and a lazy mint's root before the forest names it — the "live in-window image" — are structurally never candidates, and a retired image parked on its tail is a pending-free, not a claim. Its OWN pass beside C8 (unsharded, per volume — the class needs NODE reachability the record census never reads: one paged walk of the interior population per volume, skipped when no grant holds a claim); ONE fresh census per volume in the confirm pass. Repair `return-orphan-image-extent`: the appender FREES the orphan in ITS ring gated on its tail — the SMO retirement shape — and the cadence's `ReturnExtents` clears the bit and rewrites the record; a direct return would have left the in-window `alloc` outside the rewritten record = the `Extent` violation for the next mount. `fsck_repair_classC13`. Empty on a flat volume and an unpartitioned forest.

## 6. Found on the way (and deviations from the PR row — for adjudication)

1. **SHIPPED P0 (every layout) — the root-swap carve-out's replayed free released the LIVE root.** Chasing C13's crash window on the FLAT leg: a root swap journals no pointer record (design-smo-replay-currency §2 C′), so a kill between the swap and the next ledger record — after a D4 compaction nudge (`defrag_compact_nodes`), a merge sweep's root collapse or a threshold-maintenance root swap — leaves the mount replaying through the PREDECESSOR, live again; Option A's mount gate PARKED the swap's in-window `free(old)`, the bring-up cover's tail passed its gate, and the live root's bit was CLEARED (the probe read `root at remount: ext 0, pending 0, old_alloc false` with the INODES root at extent 0); the next claim prefers the lowest free extent, i.e. the live INODES root. Fix: `ExtentAllocator::load` DEFERS the replayed parks (bits set, FIFO untouched) and the mount pushes them once every tree is open — `park_replayed_frees(&live_roots)`, before the first post-mount SMO — dropping a free whose extent a mounted root names (`meta_kv_replay_root_frees_dropped`, WARN per drop; `RegionGrant::unpark_live_roots` for a grant). The design's §5.3.4 row ("old image parked (coverage gate)") is corrected; the wedge-remount pin's `pending == 2` and the K4 R3 harness's "the predecessor's retired extents re-enter the pool" had encoded the defect's own shape and now pin the corrected law. **The successor image stays allocated-and-unrouted** — the bounded leak the design states on a flat volume (one extent per unpublished root swap in a crash window), C13's class on a forest grant.
2. **SHIPPED (every layout) — the Reservation Report's fixed 4 KiB buffer** (the PR row's own item, KD-SYM-18): 63 extended registrants fit, the 64th and later were silently dropped. Fixed by the two-step REGCTL-sized read; proven in-process (70 registrants decode) and on the real target (128 and 1,024 reported, `pr_report_bytes` 8,256 / 65,600 B).
3. **The manager IS the D0 ladder's winner** — no separate election machinery, no lease record: `manager_lease` is derived from the ladder outcome + page 0; the successor arm is the existing ladder + the adoption of a foreign `Live` page 0's newer roots at replay. §5.8.3 for region 0 (page 0 read BEFORE the fixed-ring replay) stays PR 10's.
4. **Reactive refill beyond §5.3.3's proactive 50 % law**: a flush pass that meets `GrantExhausted` with the manager reachable refills and retries the node once in the same pass — the storm contract stalled 6 times on the proactive law alone at the floor grant (the SMO burst of one flush pass exceeds half a floor grant).
5. **Heap admission refuses `GrantExhausted` only under the unreachable-manager seam**; otherwise a commit whose leaf's grant is empty is admitted and the cadence refills — the honest shape until PR 4's lease gate carries the EWMA input.
6. **`ManagerService` is served on an `RpcListener` the contracts stand up**; the mount path does not yet start one (PR 4's lease gate is the first mount-path caller). `AcquireSlot` / `ReleaseSlot` / `OfferSlot` / `RecordDeath` / `DirRenameLock` are NOT reserved in the enum — PR 4+ extend it under schema 5 before a release, or bump again.
