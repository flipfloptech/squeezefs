## What the rung landed (product) — every finding red-first per the repro-port mandate

1. **Residual 1 — phantom-era frontier hygiene**
   (`data_alloc_lane::prune_stale_lane_records`, called by the MW arm):
   `alloc_lane:` records of widths no live claim-set era runs are pruned —
   a foreign-width record's frontier is **FOLDED into every current-width
   lane's record before the delete** (recovery clause-3 protection carried
   through; crash between fold and delete leaves both standing, the pass
   converges), and a **SOLO era deletes outright** (a solo engagement reads
   no records, and the D0 admission + the claim-set-is-the-era law prove no
   live peer of any prior width exists). Undecodable records refuse the
   ARM. Gauges `alloc_lane_stale_records_{pruned,folded}`. Pins:
   `mw_cowriter_lane_tests` ×3 (solo prune / fold-before-delete with the
   recovery floor re-checked over the post-prune records / current-width
   never touched).

2. **Residual 2 — co-writer freed-block REUSE: the lane free HARVEST**
   (`PublishCall::HarvestLaneFree`, publish schema 3 → 4). Rung 9's
   shipped free returned displaced offsets to "the free supply of lane
   `b % W`" — the AUTHORITY's free list, whose own funnel is lane-filtered,
   so that supply was reachable by NOBODY and sustained rewrite leaked
   toward ENOSPC on a store with free space. Now: at lane exhaustion
   (StorageFull inside the funnel, before the verdict) the co-writer ships
   a grain-batched harvest; the authority validates lane + era against the
   assignment IT made (`validate_lane_raise`), hands back free-listed
   indices of THAT lane — **removed from its own list (exactly-once),
   reclaim-drained once when the supply is still queued** — through a
   `(lease_epoch, request_id)` dedup window (the FreeBlocks pattern
   verbatim). The §3.1 zombie window for REUSED offsets (they sit BELOW
   the durable frontier) is closed by the **handout ledger**: every
   handout is recorded against the lease epoch, an epoch that dies with
   the reference not yet durable takes its undischarged handouts into the
   death cohort (`DeadCustody.offsets` → the S7 quarantine, drain-proof
   released), and a handout **discharges** when the offset's next shipped
   free returns it to the authority's own ladder (bounded by the peer's
   live working set, not by time). Reuse pays no reservation; non-co-writer
   mounts pay one OnceLock probe. Gauges: publish
   `harvest_{shipped,served}_blocks/_replays/_refusals` +
   `alloc_lane_harvests`/`alloc_lane_harvested_blocks`. **The charter red
   is green**: `a_co_writer_rewrite_loop_reuses_its_lanes_freed_blocks_and_
   never_hits_storagefull` (3× the lane share, `alloc_lane_enospc_refusals
   == 0`), plus the exactly-once/replay/quarantine/discharge/stale-era pins
   (`mw_cowriter_free_tests`).

3. **S9-c composition pin** (`tests/mw_colocated_fence_tests.rs`): with a
   device that structurally CANNOT reject (the co-located merged-head
   shape — the zombie's registration IS the holder's), the client-side
   gates alone carry the fencing story: a dead-epoch carried authorization
   refuses at `authorize_dma` BEFORE any device work (bytes proven
   untouched; `data_dma_epoch_refusals ⊆ data_dma_fence_refusals`), the
   DEVICE fence latch never fires, an epoch advance never poisons (re-join
   lands), and the T_self poison face is total and counted on the fence
   class alone.

**The 15 k arithmetic** (tier: arithmetic-on-measured-constants, formula
published per `docs/rc-manifest.md`): this row measures **3.6 publish
verbs/MiB** of co-writer ingest (5,536 verbs ÷ 1,536 MiB, rewrite frees
included) and the S8-a note measured the ONE authority serving **9,473
wire verbs/s** at the veth floor (owner execute ≤ 8–32 µs, so the wire
floor is the conservative bound). Composition:
`aggregate co-writer ingest bound ≈ authority_verb_ceiling ÷ 3.6 verbs/MiB
≈ 9,473 ÷ 3.6 ≈ 2.6 GiB/s` through ONE authority — **independent of
writer count**, so at 15 k writers the per-writer sustained bound is
≈ 0.18 MiB/s: the single-authority publish plane is the 15 k bottleneck
BY ARITHMETIC, which is exactly the wall S10's delegation (PR 12–14) and
KD-MW-16's fleet sharding are scheduled to move. The DATA bytes
themselves never funnel (device-direct DMA — the whole S9 design), so
the bound is metadata-plane only.

## S9-b — authority failover (GREEN from zero; `rows/s9b-1786902668`)

Per co-writer: an **fsync-acked corpus** (8 × 4 MiB urandom, sha256
recorded), then a streaming fan-out; kill -9 the authority 5 s in.

* **successor up in 1 s** over the standing WERO (fence_mode=1, the
  rung-8 takeover machinery);
* **fence scan: 2/2 co-writers fenced, zero silent old-era survivors**
  (the E2 law — S7's custody self-fence wins the race, "the client's is
  stricter");
* co-writers **re-admitted by remount** under the successor era (slot ids
  are mount-point-stable; the CONF-persistent port fix is what made the
  successor reachable);
* **zero acked-data loss**: the corpus verified byte-identical through
  the SUCCESSOR authority **and** through every re-admitted co-writer;
* oracle: fsck `findings: 0`, drift 0, `owner_panics`/`refusals`/
  `local_commit_refusals`/`invariant_tripwires` all 0.

**The re-admission verdict (S9-b's second half): DOCUMENTED DEFERRAL.**
Automatic in-place re-admission requires a production clear path for the
sticky custody poison, and *"a fenced holder is dead until remount"* is a
load-bearing safety law (every DMA gate — `authorize_dma`, the reclaim
fence halt, the pipeline fence drop — stands on its one-way latch, and a
fenced mount holds fenced-era state a resume would have to prove
coherent: staged custody, layout caches, dead-era local locks).
Un-poisoning is a designed transition needing the S6/S7/S9 planes'
adjudication, not a rung's convenience patch. The posture is now stated
on the operator page (`docs/operations.md` §Multi-writer co-writer
mounts, "Failure and re-admission", `74a2454b`): remount + supervise +
alert on the self-fence gauges. Findings #1/#2 make the fenced state
LOUD and CHEAP (instant refusals, no wedges), which is what makes the
remount posture operable.
